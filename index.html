How it works

Three steps from signup to entry

Entri replaces the whole ticketing and access workflow — no ID cards, no printed tickets, no manual checks at the gate.

Step 01
🤳

Face Verification on Signup

A live selfie is matched against a government-issued ID photo using AI. Liveness detection stops spoofing attempts before they start.

Step 02
🎟️

Identity-Locked Ticket

Tickets are issued as cryptographically hashed QR codes bound to the verified identity. They can't be transferred, duplicated, or forged.

Step 03
🚪

Face-Scan at the Gate

Attendees walk in — the camera does the rest. Instant face match marks attendance and grants entry without anyone pulling out a phone.
