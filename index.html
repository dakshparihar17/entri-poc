How it works

From sign-up to entry in three steps

Entri replaces the entire ticketing and access control workflow with a seamless, identity-first experience.

01
🤳

Live Selfie Match

A live selfie is captured at signup and compared against the government-issued ID photo using AI face recognition. Liveness detection prevents photo spoofing — no match, no access. The entire check takes under a second.

02
🎟️

Blockchain Ticket

Tickets are issued as cryptographically hashed QR codes tied directly to the holder's verified identity. They are untransferable and unforgeable — each ticket is anchored to the person who bought it, permanently.

03
🚪

Face-Scan Entry

At the gate, a camera matches the live face against the verified attendee record and marks attendance instantly — no phones out, no QR scanning, no delays. Thousands of people processed per hour with zero friction.

FAQ

Common questions, answered

Everything you need to know before deploying Entri at your venue.

How does face verification work? ▾
At signup, the attendee uploads a government-issued ID and takes a live selfie. Entri uses dlib to extract 128-dimensional face encodings from both images and computes the Euclidean distance. A distance below 0.6 confirms a match. Liveness detection ensures the selfie is taken in real time, preventing photo spoofing.
What stops someone from using a printed photo to spoof the system? ▾
Entri's liveness detection flags static images by analysing natural micro-movements and eye blink patterns during the capture window. Additionally, the gate-scan compares the live face against the stored verified encoding — not a stored photo — making it significantly harder to spoof with printed materials.
Can an attendee transfer their ticket to another person? ▾
No. Every ticket is cryptographically hashed and anchored to the identity of the person who purchased it. The hash incorporates the attendee's verified face encoding, meaning the ticket is biometrically locked. Even if a QR code were shared, the face at the gate would not match and entry would be denied.
Is biometric data stored on the cloud or third-party servers? ▾
No. Entri is designed for on-premise deployment. All face encodings, ID photos, and live selfies are stored on your own infrastructure. No biometric data is sent to third-party cloud services. You own your data entirely.
What hardware is needed at the entry gate? ▾
Any device with a standard webcam or IP camera running a browser with access to the Entri organiser dashboard. No dedicated hardware is required. A laptop or tablet at each gate is sufficient for most venues. For high-volume entry points, Entri's API supports integration with purpose-built camera kiosks.
