How it works

From sign-up to entry in three steps

Entri replaces the entire ticketing and access control workflow with a seamless, identity-first experience.

Step 01
🤳

Live Selfie Match

A live selfie is compared against the ID photo using AI face recognition. No match = no access. Liveness detection prevents spoofing.

Step 02
🎟️

Blockchain Ticket

Tickets are issued as cryptographically hashed QR codes tied to the holder's verified identity. Untransferable. Unforgeable.

Step 03
🚪

Face-Scan Entry

At the gate, a camera matches the live face and marks attendance instantly — no phones out, no scanning delays, zero friction.
