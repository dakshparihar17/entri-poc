The context

What Martyn's Law means for venues

Martyn's Law (the Terrorism (Protection of Premises) Act) places a legal duty on certain venues and events to have documented protective security measures in place. Venues meeting specified capacity thresholds must demonstrate that access controls, staff procedures, and security workflows are operational and evidenced.

Operators are already acting — the question is whether your current systems can evidence the controls the law will require. Entri gives you that evidence layer, built into your day-to-day entry operations.
